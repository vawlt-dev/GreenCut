Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated: July 2025

1. Who We Are

GreenCut is a mobile lawn and garden care service, visiting clients at their properties across the local area and surrounds.

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at {{ c.contact.email }}.

2. What Information We Collect

When you make a booking through this website, we collect:

  • Your full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address (required so we can travel to you)
  • The service you have booked, and your chosen date and time
  • Any notes you choose to include with your booking (see section 4)

We also collect standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) for security and diagnostic purposes. These are not linked to your identity.

3. Payment Information

Card payments are processed by Stripe, a PCI-DSS compliant payment provider. We never see, store, or have access to your card number, expiry date, or CVC. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to payment data: stripe.com/nz/privacy.

4. The Booking Notes Field

The notes field is intended for logistical information only — for example, gate codes, parking instructions, or access requirements. Please do not use it to submit detailed medical history or clinical information.

If you choose to include personal information in the notes field, you are doing so voluntarily and with the understanding that it will be visible to your assigned landscaper and stored as part of your booking record.

5. Who Can See Your Information

Your booking details — including your name, contact details, address, and any notes — are visible to:

  • Your assigned landscaper, who needs this information to carry out your job
  • The website administrator (the person who built and maintains this website), who has access to the booking management system for technical support purposes

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any other third parties, except as required by law.

6. Why We Collect It

We collect your personal information for the following purposes:

  • To confirm and manage your appointment
  • To contact you about your booking (confirmation, reminders, changes, or cancellations)
  • To travel to your property to carry out your lawn or garden service
  • To process payment for your appointment

7. How Long We Keep It

Booking records are retained for a minimum of 7 years in accordance with standard business record-keeping requirements. After this period, records are securely deleted.

You may request deletion of your personal details at any time after your appointments are complete, subject to our legal record-keeping obligations.

8. Security

Your data is stored on a secured server. Access to the booking system is protected by a password. Card data is handled exclusively by Stripe and is never stored on our servers.

No method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we take reasonable steps to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct any information that is inaccurate or out of date
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to our legal obligations)

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at {{ c.contact.email }}.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will reflect when it was last updated. Continued use of the website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
